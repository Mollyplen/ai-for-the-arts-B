# ai-for-the-arts-B
## Mixed-Media Digital Scrapbook

**Course:** AI for the Arts & Humanities (B), University of Glasgow
**Archive Object:**  MS Murray 167: Notes of Cruises in the “Raven” and “Wave” (1850–1866), University of Glasgow Special Collections

### Project Overview

This project reinterprets a nineteenth-century maritime journal as an interactive digital scrapbook. Rather than presenting the archive as a static document, it is designed as a navigable, visual experience that encourages users to explore, interpret, and reflect on the material.

The project combines archival images, edited extracts, and layered interpretation to make the journal more accessible to a general audience.

### Human–AI Collaboration

AI was used to support the project through:

* image enhancement and colourisation
* layout prototyping in Figma
* structuring and refining content

However, outputs were not always reliable (e.g. transcription errors and misrecognition), meaning all content was reviewed and shaped through human interpretation.
